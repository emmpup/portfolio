Music Player

Music Player

Front-End Developer

This project is a modern music player application created to give users a clean, distraction-free way to listen to their favorite tracks. Built using React, the app pulls music information and audio directly from YouTube, allowing for a massive library of content within a single interface. The design focuses on simplicity, featuring easy-to-use buttons for play, pause, and skipping tracks, all wrapped in a visually appealing layout that works smoothly on any screen.
