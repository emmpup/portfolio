# Hobby Card Animations

For my punch-up project, I'm thinking of creating playing cards with different hobbies and cool animations. I plan on using GSAP and ai to help with creating this project, since it is a little ambitious. I wanted to do this because I'm really interested in cards and wanted to incorperate that in a fun and interesting way. I'm planning on having 7 hobbies that I plan on including. I'm also debating wether to use my own pictures/videos for each hobby, or get some online, or maybe make graphics for each.

## What is the work I'm taking on?

I plan to have up to 3 major animations:

1. Fanning out/in the deck
2. Card selection and hover states
   - The card will flip when clicked, showing the hobby
3. Shuffle animation (may get rid of depending on time)

## How will this enhance my work?

This will add a new and interesting animation to my website that will help me stand out. This idea is something that I uniquely thought of, so it's something no one else will have on their site. It will also showcase advanced animation skills.
