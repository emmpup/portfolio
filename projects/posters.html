Poster Design

Advertisement Posters

Designer

This series of promotional posters showcases a magic event featuring three world-renowned performers: Shin Lim, Derren Brown, and David Copperfield. Each design utilizes a distinct color story, cool blues, mystical greens, and fiery oranges, to reflect the specific artistic persona and performance style of the magician, from sleight-of-hand to psychological mentalism and theatrical storytelling. Designed for physical print, the posters combine multi-layered photo montages with iconic magic symbols and clean typography to create an inviting, mysterious atmosphere for the audience.
